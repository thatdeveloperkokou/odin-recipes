Waakye

Description

Waakye is a popular Ghanaian dish of cooked rice and beans, often black-eyed peas or cow peas, commonly eaten for breakfast or lunch. It is known for its distinctive reddish-brown color, achieved by cooking with red sorghum leaf sheaths or stalks and limestone.

This is one of the best african dish you would ever taste. Preferably my favorite dish. Shout out to my Togolese and Ghanaian people for this masterpiece

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of Rice
  • 1 tsp of Baking SOda
  • 2-3 Dried millet leaves or sorghum leaves
  • water
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice thoroughly until the water runs clear. Soak them in water for at least 6 hours or overnight.
  2. Soak the black-eyed peas in a separate bowl in water for about 6 hours or overnight.
  3. Drain the soaked rice and set aside.
  4. Pour the soaked black-eyed peas to the pot
  5. Fill the pot with enough water to cover the beans, and add the baking soda. And let it cook for 25 minutes.
  6. Pour in the rice, If you have dried millet leaves or sorghum leaves, add them to the pot for color.
  7. Place the pot on high heat and bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for about 20minutes, or until the grains and peas are tender.
  8. Check the water level and add more to prevent burning or sticking. The waakye should have a brownish color.
  9. Remove it from the heat and let it cool slightly once the waakye is cooked. Remove the millet leaves if used.
  10. Serve the waakye with your choice of toppings, such as fried plantains, spaghetti, and shito sauce. Enjoy!
